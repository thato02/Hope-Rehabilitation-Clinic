WELCOME TO HRC

A place for support, healing and recovery

At Hope Rehabilitation Clinic, we provide professional, compassionate and confidential support to individuals and families affected by substance use. Our goal is to help people build healthier lives through counselling, rehabilitation, aftercare and prevention programs. Whether you are seeking help for yourself or a loved one, our dedicated team is here to guide you every step of the way.

WHAT WE OFFER

Our services

We offer different forms of support to meet people at different stages of their recovery journey.

01

Individual counselling

Personalised one-on-one support to help individuals understand their challenges and work towards recovery.

02

Family counselling

Helping families improve communication, rebuild relationships and understand the recovery process.

03

Rehabilitation

Structured programs designed to support individuals working towards overcoming substance dependence.

04

Aftercare support

Continued guidance following treatment to encourage long-term recovery and help prevent relapse.

05

Prevention and awareness

Educational initiatives that raise awareness and encourage healthier choices within communities.

06

Referral services

Connecting individuals with additional healthcare and specialised support when needed.
