Our Services⚒️

At Hope Rehabilitation Clinic, we offer a range of professional services to support individuals and families affected by substance use.

Individual counselling🧍‍♀️

One-on-one counselling sessions to help clients understand their challenges, develop coping skills, and work towards recovery.

Price: R450 per session

Family Counselling👨🏻‍👩🏼‍👧🏻‍👦🏻

Support for families to improve communication, rebuild relationships, and understand the recovery process.

Price: R600 per session

Rehabilitation Program🫂

Structured treatment programs designed to help individuals overcome substance dependence in a safe and supportive environment.

Price: R3 500 per program

Aftercare Support❤️

Continued guidance and support after treatment to help maintain long-term recovery and prevent relapse.

Price: R350 per session

Prevention and Awareness Programs🚭

Educational workshops and community outreach initiatives that promote healthy lifestyles and raise awareness about substance abuse.

Price: R1 500 per workshop

Referral Services📩

Referrals to healthcare providers, social services, and other specialised support when additional assistance is needed.

Price: Free
